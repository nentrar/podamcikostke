Roleplaying in a World of Ancient Magic and Forgotten Dangers

In the realm of shadows, where ancient magic seeps through the cracks in reality and forgotten gods stir in their slumber, you are called to adventure. The Kingdom of Eldara stands on the brink of darkness, and only you and your companions can prevent its fall.

Shadow Realms is a dark fantasy tabletop roleplaying game that takes you on epic quests through mysterious lands. Choose from unique character classes like the Shadow Knight, the Arcane Scholar, or the Rogue of Twilight. Navigate political intrigue in the capital city, delve into ancient dungeons filled with terrible secrets, and face creatures that should have remained in legend.

The game features an innovative magic system based on the phases of the moon, where spellcasters must carefully time their most powerful incantations. Combat is tactical and deadly, encouraging players to think strategically rather than charge headlong into battle. Death is always lurking in the shadows.

The core rulebook includes a complete campaign setting, detailed histories of the Five Kingdoms, maps of key locations, and dozens of ready-to-play adventures. Whether you're exploring the Haunted Marshes of Sorrowmere or navigating the treacherous politics of the Royal Court, every session promises danger, mystery, and memorable storytelling.

About the Creator

Created by award-winning game designer Alexandra Thornwood, Shadow Realms draws inspiration from classic fantasy literature, Nordic mythology, and years of experience running epic campaigns.

Alexandra has been crafting immersive gaming experiences for over 15 years, with multiple award-winning titles to her name. Shadow Realms represents the culmination of her vision for dark fantasy roleplaying – a world where player choices matter, magic comes with a price, and every adventure could be your last.

The stunning artwork throughout the game is provided by internationally acclaimed fantasy artist Marcus Blackwell, whose evocative paintings perfectly capture the mysterious and dangerous atmosphere of the Shadow Realms.
