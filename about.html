O mnie

I'm just a simple man

Founded in 2011, we are a game studio and publisher dedicated to creating award-winning tabletop roleplaying games and books set in strange and wondrous worlds. Based in [Your City], we've built a reputation for combining compelling narratives, innovative mechanics, and stunning artwork.

What started as a passion project among friends has grown into a celebrated publishing house, recognized by players and critics worldwide. We've been voted Fan Favorite Publisher multiple times and continue to push the boundaries of what tabletop gaming can be.

Our games transport players to extraordinary worlds where every choice matters and every story is unique. From dark fantasy to science fiction, from Nordic horror to epic adventures, we create experiences that bring people together around the table.
